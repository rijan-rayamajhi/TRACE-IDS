## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1: section_completeness
Every section and sub-section of the approved outline must exist in the draft with real prose, not headings alone. No `[MATERIAL GAP]` marker may remain unresolved when the draft is handed on. If a gap cannot be closed from the materials provided, I must report it explicitly in the self-scoring rather than leave a placeholder hidden in the text.

### D2: citation_density
Every factual statement about prior work, datasets, methods or theory carries a citation to an entry from the provided corpus. Every number about this study's own results is tied to the paper's own experiments. If a claim cannot be sourced, I either drop it or mark it `[MATERIAL GAP]`. Softening it with hedging words does not count as support. Novelty or absence claims use the search-bounded form and name the closest prior work, because an absence cannot be cited.

### D3: argument_blueprint_fidelity
Each section carries out the claim–evidence–reasoning chains of the argument blueprint that will be supplied. Topic sentences state that chain's claim, the evidence comes from the sources assigned to it, and the explanation connects back to the section's purpose. I may not add new claims, drop chains, or strengthen a claim beyond what the blueprint states.

### D4: total_word_count
The body of the draft must land within ±10% of the 7,000-word target, i.e. 6,300–7,700 words. The abstract and reference list are not counted.

### D5: per_section_word_count
Each section must stay within ±15% of its allocated length in the configuration/outline, so that no section balloons at the expense of another.

### D6: paragraph_structure
Each body paragraph makes one clear point and supports it with cited or own-result evidence. It explains why that evidence matters and links to the surrounding argument. This is judged by clarity, not by a length template, and adjacent sections must have explicit transitions.

### D7: register_consistency
The academic register for computer-security / machine-learning writing holds throughout: precise, plain, active where natural, with no colloquialisms or promotional language. Terminology stays consistent from start to finish.
